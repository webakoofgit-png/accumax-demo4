- Home and About share a company profile. About includes the published mission, vision and values.
- Industry descriptions connect the published sectors with relevant electrical services. They describe applications, rather than completed projects or certified site capabilities.
- Quality copy follows the published commitments. No new certifications or third-party ratings were added.
- The service index download, catalogue copy, career introduction and video presentation copy were refreshed. The downloaded index remains a service summary, not a claimed official product datasheet.
- The experienced-staff counter now uses the official site's `100%`, rather than `100+`.
